# Marketing site (Home + Lumen, Anchor, BOLT, FIST product pages)

Scope: the public marketing site. Mode: Persuade. Audience: military buyers first, investors second (see PRODUCT.md).
Job: understand the four-product system and ask for a conversation. Primary action: Contact; secondary: explore a product.
Proof on hand: the three product GLB models. No customers, benchmarks or press items yet. On 2026-10-23 the user removed the sunrise footage and the BOLT launch clip from the site and asked for the Paper hero instead, made live.
Constraints: copy comes from product-docs/ and the approved Paper file; the Anchor stays closed in 3D; all copy claims stay inside PRODUCT.md.

Visual authority: the approved Paper design (file 01M5JMTGY14Y8YWJPGPG0ATF53, page "Website"). This build transfers that world. It is not a new direction round.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The low sky at dawn, defended: an airy, editorial site where the Paper sky, made live, and the real hardware do the persuading. It refuses the category default of dark tactical HUD pages with neon glows and stock-soldier photos.

OWN-WORLD: Pre-dawn sky ground (#E8EDF1 to #F4EEE6), ink #0D1117, slate #56606B, night panels #0A0F16, and one ember #FF5B1F used only for live signals. Newsreader Light display with tight tracking; Geist body; Geist Mono uppercase labels. 14px-radius panels inset 10px from the viewport, hairline spec rows, white or ink pill buttons ending in a square arrow chip, and a stacked side nav of tinted blocks running dawn to night. Products render as matte-black 3D objects on calm gradient grounds.

STORY: The visitor sees the sky they must defend, reads the threats, follows one sensor-to-shot loop, compares four products, explores each one in 3D, and makes contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A rounded hero panel takes the left ~88% of the viewport, with the open rail to its right; it collapses to a strip of its colours, with a section marker and previews, once the visitor scrolls. Inside it, a WebGL scene renders the Paper sky (night to warm horizon) over drifting shader cumulus banks, with faint range rings and the site marker below. "The Low Sky, Defended" sits centred in the upper third (128px Newsreader Light, white), the FTI mark is top-left, a live COUNTER-UAS SYSTEMS label is top-right, and the white "Explore products" pill is bottom-centre. Scrolling plays one illustrative engagement: drones climb out of the clouds, Lumen brackets lock and classify them, the operator approves, two ember trajectories draw from the site, BOLT hits the second track from the left, and FIST hits the delta wing. A Detect, Classify, Approve, Intercept stepper tracks the sequence. Nothing flies in the first frame. At the end of the hero the sky brightens into the page's own gradient and the panel dissolves into the section below with no seam.

FORM: Inherited world, a faithful transfer of the user-approved Paper design. There was no concept roll (seed key: none, inherited). Signature interactions: the scroll-driven engagement in the cloud hero, and 3D choreography for each product. BOLT tips from upright in its tube onto its flight path; FIST's propellers spin as it banks; the Anchor turns to show its rugged corners. Every model can be dragged to rotate. Motion grammar: scroll-linked and eased, never springy; no blanket section entrances; reduced motion shows final states.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
